# Evidence rules

Each signal supports a narrow claim. Word findings at the level the signal supports.

| Signal | Supports | Word it as |
|---|---|---|
| Material moving between stations | a piece moved | "a piece moved from the queue to B", not "a garment was finished" |
| Hand in the keypad zone near a CP capture | the capture is consistent with B keying it | "matches B's hand on the keypad (−1.2 s)", not "B pressed the key" |
| Head orientation | where the head points | "head turned to the queue", not attention, intent or productivity |
| Deposit into an off-camera basket | inferred end of cycle | "inferred deposit" |
| Track through occlusion | continuity only when the track is unbroken | split and flag the gap |
| 3D pose / 3D objects | estimates | "estimated 3D", shown apart from the video |
| CP quantity × SAM | efficiency at the CP clock | state the assumptions (operators, SAM covers sewing only) |
| A capture with no cycle, or a cycle with no capture | a mismatch to review | "para revisar", never an accusation |

State the chance rate next to every agreement number (e.g. 34 of 35 captures match; chance 43 %).
Confidence per finding: alta, media or baja, with the reason.
