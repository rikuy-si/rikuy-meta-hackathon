# Rikuy voice and facts

## Voice

- Clear and specific, like a store owner talks: numbers, hours, cameras, tickets.
- "Look after", never "watch over". The video also protects the honest employee.
- Short sentences, active voice. No filler adjectives (powerful, revolutionary, intelligent).
- Words to use: see, look, check, answer, clip, record, camera, night shift, owner, your agent, on the record.
- Words never to use: surveillance, spy, monitor (your employees), all-seeing, Big Brother, "AI vision intelligence".
- Rikuy is software for operations and fraud detection. Never describe Rikuy as a security or monitoring service. "Security cameras" only names the hardware the owner already has.
- Name what the agent does, not what Rikuy does: Rikuy gives access to the video with permissions and a record; the agent interprets. Write examples as questions to the agent, not "Rikuy detects".
- Customer-facing text is English. Internal docs for the team are Spanish.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere.
- Explain jargon once: MCP (Model Context Protocol) is how an AI agent uses outside tools; it is Rikuy's door.

## Lines

- Brand line: **Seen, not guessed.**
- Warmer alternative: See. Notice. Know.
- Site headline: **We help your AI agents see what's relevant to you.**
- Campaign headline: Your cameras record everything. Nobody watches.
- Investor descriptor (only for investors): Business superintelligence through your cameras.
- Owner descriptor: Business intelligence through your cameras.
- The ladder (use in this order): Protect (what happened that should not have), Operate (how the store is running), Grow (what am I not seeing). "Security is why you installed them. It's not all they can tell you."
- "You don't have to know what to ask. Your agent notices what matters."

## What we can say

- The cameras you have, the AI you use. No new cameras, no new recorder.
- Rikuy is software, never hardware: the owner's AI agent installs it on a computer at the business and adds the Rikuy connector, or walks the owner through it. Nothing to buy, nothing to change on the router. Video stays on the recorder. Always explain it simply, without technical words.
- Works with Muse, Claude and Grok at launch; ChatGPT, Gemini and the rest after. Any MCP client can already add the pre-launch server (https://mcp.rikuy.si/mcp): it answers questions about Rikuy and sees no camera until launch.
- Recorders planned at launch: Hikvision and Dahua (Lorex included), then Reolink. Cameras that only offer RTSP or ONVIF: live view only, later.
- Pre-launch: a free private pilot with a few small businesses. Owners join the early access list at https://rikuy.si/start/.
- Hard rules: no audio, no restroom or changing-area cameras, no face recognition, video stays on the recorder, every look is on the record, one tap cuts all access.
- Prices: not defined yet. Never state or invent prices or plan names.
- Owner approves each agent in their own browser (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE); agents never receive passwords or card data.

## What we cannot say

- No claims of detecting age, gender or demographics, or of recognizing faces.
- No safety promises for homes, children or pools.
- No invented customers, quotes, percentages, savings or awards. If it is not in this file or on rikuy.si, write `[sin dato]` and ask.
- No "monitoring" or "security service" positioning. No comparison that names a competitor unless the user supplies the facts.
- Do not claim affiliation with any company. (The shorthand "Tailscale for your cameras, built for AI agents" is only for the investors page, with the line "not affiliated with Tailscale Inc.")

## Example questions (Protect, Operate, Grow)

- How many scratch-offs left the counter, and how many does the lottery terminal show?
- How long did the line at the register get between 5 and 7 PM?
- At which hours do people walk in and leave without buying?
